# College Education Across North Carolina

This interactive web map shows the distribution of colleges and universities across North Carolina and compares the number of mapped institutions in each county.

Counties are shaded in different shades of blue based on the number of college and university locations within their boundaries. Darker blue represents more institutions, while lighter blue represents fewer. Orange graduation-cap symbols identify individual college and university locations.

Users can click on college markers to view institution information and click on counties to explore their mapped college counts. The map also includes a legend, scale bar, basemap, and source credits.

The project was developed using HTML, JavaScript, Leaflet, and GeoJSON. County boundaries come from Rowan County GIS / North Carolina Geodetic Survey, and college locations come from NC OneMap's historical 2007 dataset. County counts were calculated from these mapped college locations, so they may not reflect the current number of institutions.
